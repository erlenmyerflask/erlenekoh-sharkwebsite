FUN FACTS

  1. There are at least nine species of hammerhead shark in the world, although some studies may suggest more.
  2. The distinctive 'hammer-head' part of the shark is called a cephalofoil.It's responsible for the distinctive shape of the hammerhead group and serves many purposes including giving them superior depth perception and binocular vision
  3. The position of one eye at each end of a hammerhead shark's head allows them to have a complete 360-degree range of vision. However this comes with a blind spot right in front of their nose.
  4. Hammerheads have only been around about 20-25 million years and are newer evolved shark species compared to the rest which have been around for at least 420 million years.
  5. Hammerheads like to be in shallow waters and as a result, some species have actually developed the ability to tan. Their skin may turn from light grey to dark grey
