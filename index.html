BASIC FACTS

  1. The scientific name for sharks is Selachimorpha
  2. They generally have a lifespan of 25-30 years
  3. There are more than 465 different types of sharks
  4. Sharks don't have bones. Their skeletons are made of cartilage which is the same soft, flexible tissue as your ears and the tip of your nose
  5. Sharks have been around in the oceans for over 400 million years
