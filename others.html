Shark Fun Fact Picture
Great White Shark Great white sharks can be found throughout the world's oceans, mostly in cool waters close to the coast
Lemon Shark Lemon sharks are named for their yellow-brown skin that helps them camouflage in the sandy, tropical waters they inhabit
Blacktip Reef Shark Blacktip reef sharks primarily feed on reef fish but sometimes eat crustaceans, cephalopods and mollusks.
