FUN FACTS

  1. They are also the world's largest fish, usually growing to 12m in length, but they can reach up to 20m long and weigh 20 tonnes
  2. Whale sharks are filter feeders and can neither bite nor chew
  3. They can process more than 6,000 litres of water an hour through their gills.
  4. Although their mouths can stretch to four feet wide(roughly 1.2m), their teeth are very tiny so they only eat small shrimp, fish and plankton by using their gill rakers as a suction filter.
  5. It's thought that less than 10% of whale sharks born survive to adulthood, but those that do may live to 150.
